title: "Feature List"
---

Simplify Roblox nametags with Nametags by Noren, a flexible system designed to make managing player nametags easier. Add attribute icons, use the API to set attribute text, and shine with rainbow usernames. Set up your system with its easy to customise whitelists. Built to keep nametags powerful without making them complicated.

![Nametags 1](/images/Nametags-1.png "Nametags 1")

- **Built for Integration** — Display additional text from your own systems, giving you more ways to surface important information directly on every nametag.
- **Badges That Stand Out** — Add vibrant, colourful badges that make every nametag instantly recognisable.
- **Rainbow, Reimagined** — Bring usernames to life with a vibrant rainbow effect that turns heads.
- **Your Rules, Your Whitelist** — Easily customise whitelists and control exactly who gets access to exclusive features.
- **Reward Your Supporters** — Give Gamepass purchasers exclusive nametag features as a premium perk.
- **Personalisation, Coming Soon** — A powerful customisation UI is coming soon, making nametag management simpler than ever.
- **JSM EAS Integration, Coming Soon** — Seamless integration with JSM EAS, unlocking yet another way to power your nametags.
